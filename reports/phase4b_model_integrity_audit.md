**Encoding:** one-hot against the fixed 7-day list, Monday dropped as the reference category (6 dummy columns) — unchanged from Phase 3A.8/4A.

**Overall distribution (175 common-window rows):** Friday 27, Saturday 26, Monday 25, Tuesday 25, Wednesday 25, Thursday 24, Sunday 23. **All 7 days are represented** — no category is entirely absent anywhere in the dataset.

**Findings, labeled exactly as the task requires:**
- **VERIFIED:** every fold's training range contains at least one example of every weekday — no fold trains on a category with zero representation.
- **LIMITATION:** Sunday's training-fold count (13–17 out of 105–133 rows, i.e. ~12–13% of each fold) is small in absolute terms. A dummy-variable coefficient estimated from this few examples inherently carries a wider plausible range of "true" values than one estimated from a category with, say, 40+ examples.
- **CAUTION:** the large positive Sunday coefficient Phase 4A flagged (+73.16 in that phase's single-fold inspection) is directly consistent with, and plausibly explained by, this limited representation. This audit does not resolve whether Sunday has a real effect on Hyderabad district potato prices — it documents that the sample size behind that coefficient is small enough that the question remains genuinely open (Section 6 quantifies exactly how much this coefficient actually moves across folds).

**Nothing about the feature was changed.** `day_of_week` was neither removed nor re-encoded; this section is a documentation-only audit, as instructed.

**On the three features this task specifically asked for attention to:**

- **`lag_3`:** stable sign (always positive) across all 5 folds, mean +33.31 with a moderate stdev (7.08) relative to its magnitude — this is the most consistently-signed and, along with `rolling_mean_3`, the most trustworthy-looking numeric coefficient in the table.
- **`rolling_mean_3`:** the single most stable coefficient of all eleven — stable sign, and by far the smallest stdev relative to its mean (1.59 vs. a mean of 20.99, a coefficient of variation under 8%). ## 9. Model Interpretation

**Building on Phase 4A's own mean-reversion finding (Section 13 there), this phase's coefficient-stability results (Section 6) allow a more precise interpretation than Phase 4A's single-fold snapshot could support:**

The frozen model's validation performance appears to be driven by a **combination**, not any single mechanism:
1. **Regression toward the training-period mean** remains part of the story — the intercept in standardized-feature space still anchors predictions near the training mean, exactly as Phase 4A found, and this alone explains why the model beats persistence on a weakly-autocorrelated series (Phase 3A.8).
2. **A genuine, stable lag/smoothing contribution** — `lag_3` and especially `rolling_mean_3` show the most stable coefficients in this entire audit (Section 6), consistent sign across every fold, and `rolling_mean_3` in particular has a coefficient of variation under 8%. This is real evidence, not proof of causation, that recent price *level* (smoothed over 3 observations, and echoed 3 steps back) carries a repeatable statistical association with the next observation.
3. **A calendar-effect component that this audit cannot currently validate as robust** — the `day_of_week` coefficients, especially `dow_Sunday` (shrinking substantially as more data accumulates) and `dow_Thursday` (changing sign entirely), show the instability pattern of an under-sampled, noise-influenced set of estimates rather than a demonstrated market pattern.

**Observed predictive association vs. economic causation — kept explicitly distinct:** this audit shows that `lag_3`, `rolling_mean_3`, and (with much lower confidence) `day_of_week` are *statistically associated* with next-observation modal price within this specific 175-row, six-month, district-level-proxy sample. **This is not a claim that any of these variables *causes* the next price**, nor a claim that a discovered market mechanism (e.g., "Sunday mandi closures suppress reported prices") has been established — such a claim would require domain-specific verification (e.g., confirming actual mandi operating calendars) this project has not performed. The correct, limited claim is: *these are the associations a linear model finds in this sample, some more stable across resampling than others, and stability is not the same thing as proof of a real-world mechanism.*

## 10. Limitations

- **This audit operates entirely within the train+validation pool (rows [0:140])** — nothing here says anything about how the frozen model will perform on the still-untouched 35-row test set; that remains genuinely unknown until a later phase's single, final test evaluation.
- **The coefficient-stability check (Section 6) uses only 5 folds**, each built from a training window that shares nearly all of its data with its neighbors (each successive fold's training set is a superset of the previous) — this means the 5 coefficient estimates are not 5 independent samples; some of their apparent stability (or instability) partly reflects how much new data each fold actually added (7 rows out of 105–133), not fully independent re-estimation.
- **The day-of-week findings in this audit (Sections 4, 6) identify a caution; they do not resolve it.** A dedicated correlation or significance check for `day_of_week` against the target — never performed at any point in this project (Phase 3A.8 explicitly deferred it) — remains outside this audit-only phase's scope.
- **Reproducibility was confirmed on this machine, this Python/scikit-learn environment, and this exact code path.** A different environment (e.g., a different scikit-learn version with a different internal solver for `LinearRegression`) is not guaranteed to reproduce to the same sub-cent precision, though the underlying closed-form OLS solution should still match to a practically identical degree.

## 11. Freeze Decision

**No critical failure was found:**
- Feature representation: 0 mismatches against an independent recomputation from raw data.
- Reproducibility: confirmed to within floating-point precision (max difference 0.0048).
- Leakage: all 5 checks (training/validation ordering, target alignment, no validation-in-training, scaler scope, test-boundary respect) confirmed.

**DECISION: THE MODEL IS FROZEN.**

**Frozen specification:** Extended feature set (`lag_1`, `lag_2`, `lag_3`, `rolling_mean_3`, `rolling_std_3`, `day_of_week`) + plain Linear Regression + per-fold `StandardScaler`, mean validation MAE 147.54, mean validation RMSE 193.32, per the 5-fold expanding-window protocol of Phase 3A.9/4A.

**Per this phase's own rule, now binding going forward:** no further feature engineering, model selection, hyperparameter tuning, or validation-protocol modification may occur before the final test evaluation. The `day_of_week` caution (Sections 4, 6) is carried forward as a documented, open limitation of the frozen model — not a reason to alter it now.
